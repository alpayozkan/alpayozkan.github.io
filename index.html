Alpay Özkan

Hi! I'm a Data Science graduate of ETH Zurich, focusing on 3D Vision, Mapping and Robot Perception.

I am currently an autonomy engineering intern at Skydio in San Mateo, working on visual mapping and 3D reconstruction for autonomous drones under Dr. Bargoti. Before that, I was a research intern at Align Technology in Zurich, working on 3D dental reconstruction and generative models for orthodontic planning.

At ETH, I was part of the Computer Vision and Geometry Group headed by Prof. Dr. Marc Pollefeys, where I worked closely with Dr. Daniel Barath, Jelena Trisovic, Dr. Rémi Pautrat and Shaohui Liu on adaptive 3D mapping and monocular plane segmentation. My Master's thesis there resulted in UNRL, an uncertainty-aware, RL-controlled adaptive 3D mapping method, accepted at BMVC 2026, and our work on pixel-wise planarity for monocular plane segmentation was accepted at ECCV 2026. During my studies I spent a research internship at Disney Research Zurich under Dr. Tunç Aydın, working on image matting for hybrid hand-drawn and CG animation.

Before starting my MSc, I did my BSc in Computer Engineering at Middle East Technical University in Ankara, graduating 7th of 299, with an exchange year at Aalto University. I was a research intern at the Computer Vision Laboratory of Linköping University under Prof. Dr. Michael Felsberg and Dr. Abdelrahman Eldesokey, and at the Kovan Robotics Lab under Prof. Dr. Erol Şahin and Prof. Dr. Sinan Kalkan. My undergraduate research with Prof. Dr. Sinan Kalkan and Prof. Dr. Emre Akbaş on class imbalance and uncertainty resulted in the Class Uncertainty preprint.

Uncertainty-Aware 3D Mapping

Alpay Özkan. Supervised by Prof. Marc Pollefeys, advised by Dr. Daniel Barath and Jelena Trisovic

MSc thesis · ETH Zurich, Sep 2025 Grade 6.0 / 6.0

Extends MapAdapt's quality-adaptive TSDF mapping so that voxels are subdivided where semantic predictions are uncertain or where the surface is texture-rich, with Bayesian semantic fusion and multi-resolution mesh extraction. Evaluated on HSSD and ScanNet.
